Lesson 06 · Services & Repositories

Domain services, application services, and DTOs

Where does the code go? Disentangle your services: Application Services for orchestration, Domain Services for business logic, and DTOs for safe data transfer.

Time ~10 min Win Differentiate between Application Services, Domain Services, and Data Transfer Objects Mission link Prevents bloated god-services by establishing clear service boundaries

1Disentangling the Service Layer

In many systems, everything is dumped into a massive UserService class that handles password hashing, sending emails, running SQL queries, and calculating billing. This creates bloated, untestable god-objects.

Domain-Driven Design (DDD) divides services into two distinct roles: Application Services (thin coordinators that orchestrate security, transactions, and notification dispatch) and Domain Services (pure business logic, like interest rate calculations, with zero I/O or email knowledge). DTOs (Data Transfer Objects) carry data safely across boundaries.

Key idea Application services orchestrate workflows and I/O; Domain services execute pure business calculations.

2The Service Layer hierarchy

Observe how Application Services coordinate Domain Services and Repositories.

5Do this next

  3. Ensure your Domain Services have zero dependencies on notification or email libraries.
