Lesson 03 · DNS Records & Propagation

Record types: A, CNAME, MX, and TXT

A zone file is a phonebook with different entry types. Learn when to use A, AAAA, CNAME, MX, and TXT records without breaking email or apex domains.

Time ~10 min Win Configure standard DNS record types accurately and avoid the CNAME apex restriction Mission link Essential for deploying web services, configuring custom domains, and setting up email

1The core DNS vocabulary

A domain zone file is a list of resource records. Each record has a type: A maps a name to an IPv4 address, AAAA maps to an IPv6 address, CNAME aliases one name to another, MX routes email to mail servers, and TXT stores arbitrary text for domain verification and security.

A critical rule: A CNAME record cannot exist at the root apex domain (e.g. example.com) because RFC 1034 forbids CNAMEs from coexisting with other records like MX or NS.

Key idea A points to IPv4; CNAME aliases to another domain; CNAME cannot live at the zone apex.
