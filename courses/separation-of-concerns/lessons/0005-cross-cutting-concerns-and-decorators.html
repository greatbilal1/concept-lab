Lesson 05 · Cross-Cutting Concerns & Layers

Cross-cutting concerns and decorators

Logging, authentication, caching, and rate limiting don't belong inside your business logic. Learn how to extract cross-cutting concerns using Python decorators and wrapper functions.

Time ~10 min Win Extract cross-cutting concerns into reusable decorators and function wrappers Mission link Keeps domain functions focused strictly on business calculations without infrastructure clutter

1The tangle of cross-cutting concerns

A Cross-Cutting Concern is a requirement that spans across multiple modules and layers: logging, user authentication, timing metrics, input validation, and caching. If every function manually repeats 15 lines of logging and auth boilerplate, the actual 2 lines of business logic are buried.

The solution is the Decorator Pattern. In Python, decorators (@decorator) wrap around a function to inject cross-cutting behavior dynamically, leaving the core function 100% focused on business rules.

Key idea Decorators wrap functions to execute cross-cutting concerns without modifying core business code.
