1Core principles of Zero-Shot vs Few-Shot Prompting: The Power of Examples

When you ask a model to perform a task with zero examples (Zero-Shot), you are relying entirely on the model's pre-training assumptions. If you ask: 'Extract sentiment from this review', it might return 'Positive', 'Sentiment: 4/5', or 'The user was very happy'.

Key idea The extraordinary power of few-shot prompting: in-context learning, selecting exemplary pairs, and eliminating edge-case ambiguity.

2Zero-Shot vs Few-Shot Accuracy

Few-Shot Prompting (Brown et al., 2020) provides 2 to 5 concrete demonstration examples. It is the single highest-ROI technique in prompt engineering:

3Curating Diverse Examples

  • Format Grounding: Shows the exact desired casing, punctuation, and structure without needing paragraphs of explanation.
  • Edge-Case Teaching: Demonstrates how to handle tricky ambiguities (e.g. how to categorize mixed or sarcastic reviews).
  • In-Context Learning: Activates the model's internal associative representations, dramatically boosting accuracy on classification, extraction, and reasoning tasks.
# The Few-Shot Prompt Pattern:
Categorize the customer inquiry into [BILLING, TECH_SUPPORT, SALES].

Example 1:
Input: "My credit card was charged twice for subscription"
Category: BILLING

Example 2:
Input: "The login page returns a 500 error when clicking submit"
Category: TECH_SUPPORT

Example 3:
Input: "Do you offer enterprise pricing for 500 seats?"
Category: SALES

The Law of Examples: One concrete example is worth ten paragraphs of instructions. If an agent struggles with an edge case, add an example demonstrating that exact case!
