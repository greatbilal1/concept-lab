Lesson 05 · Delegation & Wrapper Patterns

The Strategy pattern: pluggable behavior

Never subclass just to change an algorithm. Master the Strategy pattern: encapsulating algorithms into swappable classes to change behavior at runtime.

Time ~10 min Win Replace rigid inheritance branching with pluggable Strategy pattern collaborators Mission link The premier Gang of Four pattern embodying 'favor composition over inheritance'

1Algorithms as swappable objects

A common beginner instinct when supporting multiple shipping calculation algorithms is subclassing: AirOrder, GroundOrder, SeaOrder. What happens when you also need different discount algorithms? You explode into AirOrderWithHolidayDiscount, GroundOrderWithVipDiscount!

The cure is the Strategy Pattern. Instead of subclassing, extract the algorithm into a family of pluggable classes: ShippingStrategy (Air, Ground, Sea). The Order simply holds a reference to a shipping strategy: order.shipping_strategy.calculate(order).

Key idea The Strategy pattern encapsulates interchangeable algorithms into pluggable collaborator objects.

2The Strategy pattern architecture

Observe how the Context class delegates its algorithm to a swappable Strategy interface.
