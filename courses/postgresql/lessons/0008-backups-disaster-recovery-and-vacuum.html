Lesson 08 · Operations & Backups

Backups, disaster recovery, and vacuum

There are two kinds of engineers: those who have lost data, and those who will. Master logical backups with pg_dump, physical Point-In-Time Recovery (PITR), and VACUUM maintenance.

Time ~10 min Win Execute database backups, perform Point-In-Time Recovery, and manage vacuum maintenance Mission link The ultimate operational safeguard protecting enterprise data from permanent loss

1Logical dumps versus Physical backups

A database backup is only as good as your ability to restore it. PostgreSQL provides two backup mechanisms: Logical Backups (pg_dump) and Physical Backups with WAL Archiving (Point-In-Time Recovery / PITR).

pg_dump exports the database as SQL scripts or a compressed custom archive. It is perfect for small-to-medium databases. For large terabyte databases, continuous WAL archiving allows Point-in-Time Recovery: you can roll back the database to any exact historical second (e.g. 1 minute before a junior developer accidentally ran DROP TABLE)!

Key idea Use pg_dump for logical backups; use WAL archiving for continuous Point-in-Time Recovery (PITR).
