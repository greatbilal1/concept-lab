Lesson 08 · Anomalies & Denormalization

Intentional denormalization and trade-offs

Normalization is for data correctness; denormalization is for read speed. Learn when, why, and how to intentionally introduce redundancy with eyes wide open.

Time ~10 min Win Evaluate and apply intentional denormalization strategies to solve proven query bottlenecks Mission link Balances theoretical purity with real-world high-throughput performance

1When 3NF meets high traffic

In pure 3NF, querying an article with its author, category, tags, and comment count requires joining five tables. At 50,000 queries per second, calculating COUNT(comments.id) on every page render will melt your database CPU.

Intentional Denormalization is the deliberate introduction of duplicate data or precomputed values (such as adding a cached_comment_count directly onto the articles table). It is not sloppy design: it is an intentional trade-off that trades write complexity for instant $O(1)$ read speed.

Key idea Denormalization trades write overhead and synchronization complexity for instant read performance.
