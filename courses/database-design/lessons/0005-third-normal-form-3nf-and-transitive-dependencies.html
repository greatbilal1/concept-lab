Third normal form (3NF) and transitive dependencies

Every non-key attribute must depend on the key, the whole key, and nothing but the key. Master 1NF, 2NF, and 3NF to eliminate duplicate data and design flaws.

Time ~10 min Win Normalize messy relational schemas into Third Normal Form (3NF) Mission link The core mathematical discipline for durable relational schema design

1The three normal forms in plain English

Database normalization is a step-by-step process to eliminate redundant data. Bill Kent summarized it in one famous sentence: 'Every non-key attribute must provide a fact about the key, the whole key, and nothing but the key, so help me Codd.'

1NF (The Key): All values are atomic; no repeating groups. 2NF (The Whole Key): No partial dependencies on composite keys. 3NF (Nothing But the Key): No transitive dependencies — columns cannot depend on other non-key columns.

Key idea 3NF ensures every non-key column depends solely on the primary key, eliminating redundancy.
