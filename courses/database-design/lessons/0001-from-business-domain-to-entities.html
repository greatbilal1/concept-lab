Lesson 01 · Domain Modeling & Cardinality

From business domain to entities

How do you turn a messy real-world problem into clean relational tables? Learn how to identify entities, attributes, and business relationships.

Time ~10 min Win Extract entities, attributes, and relationships from business requirements Mission link The initial conceptual phase of all software data modeling

1Extracting nouns and verbs

Database design begins with human language. When domain experts describe a business, their sentences hold the architecture: nouns become Entities (Tables), adjectives become Attributes (Columns), and verbs become Relationships (Foreign Keys).

'A customer places multiple orders. Each order contains several line items of products.' That single sentence defines four distinct tables and three relationships.

Key idea Domain nouns map to tables; adjectives map to columns; verbs map to relationships.
