Lesson 03 · Many-to-Many & Keys

Many-to-many relationships and junction tables

Students have many courses; courses have many students. How do you model many-to-many relationships without breaking the relational model? Master junction tables.

Time ~10 min Win Design many-to-many relationships using junction tables with composite primary keys Mission link The universal design pattern for representing multi-directional relationships

1The many-to-many dilemma

In a Many-to-Many (N:M) relationship, entities on both sides can have multiple partners: an Article has multiple Tags, and a Tag applies to multiple Articles. Neither table can hold the foreign key without storing invalid arrays.

The relational solution is an intermediary Junction Table (also called a join or pivot table). The junction table decomposes one N:M relationship into two 1:N relationships: Article (1) → ArticleTags (N) ← (1) Tag.

Key idea A junction table converts an impossible N:M relationship into two clean 1:N relationships.
