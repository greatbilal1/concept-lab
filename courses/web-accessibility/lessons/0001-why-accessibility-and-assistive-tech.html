Lesson 01 · Accessibility Foundations & Assistive Tech

Why Accessibility: Assistive Tech & The Law

Over one billion people experience disabilities. Learn how assistive technologies interact with websites and the legal landscape of accessibility.

Time ~10 min Win Articulate the four WCAG principles (POUR) and explain how assistive technology navigates web applications Mission link Comply with global accessibility legislation and build software welcoming to every user

1Disabilities are human realities, not edge cases

Disability is part of being human. Impairments fall across four broad categories:

  • Visual: Blindness, low vision, color blindness, cataracts.
  • Motor / Mobility: Tremors, paralysis, RSI, cerebral palsy, or temporary injuries (broken arm).
  • Auditory: Deafness, hard of hearing.
  • Cognitive & Neurological: Dyslexia, ADHD, autism, memory loss, seizure disorders.

Accessibility also benefits situational contexts: operating a phone in glaring sunlight, holding a baby with one arm, or watching a muted video in a quiet library.

Key idea Accessibility is universal design: solving for permanent disabilities directly improves experiences for situational limitations.

2The legal and compliance landscape

Accessibility is no longer optional. Global legislation strictly enforces WCAG 2.1/2.2 Level AA compliance:

  • Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA Title II / III): Applied to digital websites as public accommodations; thousands of lawsuits are filed annually against inaccessible sites.
  • Section 508: Mandates accessible technology for all US federal agencies and contractors.
  • European Accessibility Act (EAA): Mandates digital accessibility across e-commerce, banking, and digital services across the EU.
