Lesson 06 · Mental Models & Tests

Skimming versus deep reading

Knowing what to ignore is the superpower of fast comprehension. Learn how to skim for structure and only deep-read when you hit the critical seam.

Time ~10 min Win Categorize code into skimmable plumbing and critical business logic Mission link Dramatically increases comprehension speed by filtering noise

1Selective attention

A typical 500-line file contains about 50 lines of core business logic. The remaining 450 lines are boilerplate: error checking, logging, parameter serialization, imports, and plumbing.

If you read at full attention the entire time, mental fatigue sets in within thirty minutes. You must learn to skim the plumbing with low attention, slowing down to deep read only when you reach the core decision point.

Key idea Skim the plumbing quickly; invest your deep attention only in the core decision logic.

2The two gear speeds

Operate in two distinct reading speeds: Gear 1 (Skimming for shape and landmarks) and Gear 2 (Deep reading for correctness and state mutation).

3Filtering boilerplate

Observe how scanning past defensive checks isolates the single line where the key calculation occurs.

4Practice

Test your skill at distinguishing plumbing from core logic.

✓ Your win for today

You can survey large files in seconds and zero in on the small percentage of code that actually matters.

5Do this next

  1. Open a large file in your repo and highlight the boilerplate versus domain logic.
  2. Practice skimming a 300-line module in under two minutes to describe its purpose.
  3. Locate the single line where an essential decision is made.
