Status codes and what they mean

Stop returning 200 OK with error messages inside the body. Master the five status code families and the exact codes that speak clear truth to clients.

Time ~10 min Win Select and interpret standard HTTP status codes across the 2xx, 3xx, 4xx, and 5xx families Mission link Enables proper error handling and automated client retry policies

1The five numerical families

HTTP status codes are grouped into five distinct families by their first digit: 1xx (Informational), 2xx (Success), 3xx (Redirection), 4xx (Client Error), and 5xx (Server Error).

The crucial distinction is between 4xx and 5xx. A 4xx error means the client did something wrong (bad parameters, unauthorized, resource missing). A 5xx error means the server crashed, timed out, or encountered an unexpected internal defect.

Key idea 4xx errors are the client's fault; 5xx errors are the server's fault.
