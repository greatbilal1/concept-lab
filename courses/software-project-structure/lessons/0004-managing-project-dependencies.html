Lesson 04 · Configuration & Secrets

Managing project dependencies

Dependencies change under your feet unless locked down. Learn the difference between abstract manifests and concrete lockfiles for reproducible builds.

Time ~10 min Win Differentiate manifests and lockfiles to achieve 100% reproducible builds Mission link Eliminates 'works on my machine' dependency failures

1Manifests versus lockfiles

A dependency management system has two distinct jobs handled by two separate files. The manifest (e.g. pyproject.toml or package.json) defines your abstract requirements: 'I need FastAPI version 0.100 or newer.'

The lockfile (e.g. poetry.lock or package-lock.json) records the concrete, exact resolution: 'FastAPI 0.104.1 with sha256 checksum abc... and Pydantic 2.4.2.'

Key idea Manifests declare what you want; lockfiles record the exact cryptographic resolution of what was installed.
