Lesson 03 · Outer Joins & Missing Data

Full outer joins and anti-joins

How do you find what ISN'T there? Learn the FULL OUTER JOIN for total reconciliations, and the Anti-Join pattern for finding customers who never bought anything.

Time ~10 min Win Find missing relationships using anti-joins and reconcile datasets with FULL OUTER JOIN Mission link The primary pattern for auditing data discrepancies and churned users

1Finding the missing pieces

A common business question is negative: 'Find all users who have NEVER placed an order', or 'Find all products that have NEVER been purchased'. How do you query the absence of data?

The answer is an Anti-Join. You perform a LEFT JOIN from users to orders, and then add a WHERE orders.id IS NULL filter. This discards all matching customers and retains only customers with zero orders!

Key idea An anti-join (LEFT JOIN ... WHERE right.id IS NULL) finds records that have zero matching children.

2FULL OUTER JOIN reconciliation

A FULL OUTER JOIN returns all rows from both tables, pairing matches and inserting NULLs on both sides.
