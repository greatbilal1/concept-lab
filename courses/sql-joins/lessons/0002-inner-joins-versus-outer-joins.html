Lesson 02 · Outer Joins & Missing Data

Inner joins versus outer joins

Where did my customers go? Discover why INNER JOIN silently drops records, and how LEFT JOIN preserves every row from your primary table with NULL padding.

Time ~10 min Win Select between INNER and LEFT joins to preserve records with optional relationships Mission link Eliminates the most common bug in business reporting queries: dropped rows

1The tragedy of the dropped row

A manager asks: 'List all customers and their total order count.' You write an INNER JOIN. Your query looks great, but your report shows 800 customers when the database has 1,000 customers. What happened?

The 200 new customers who have not placed an order yet were silently erased from your report! When relationships are optional, you must use a LEFT OUTER JOIN: it returns all rows from the left table, inserting NULL for the right table columns if no match exists.

Key idea Use LEFT JOIN whenever child records are optional, ensuring parent rows are never dropped.
