Lesson 08 · OAuth & Modern Authentication

Multi-factor authentication and passkeys

Passwords are dying. Explore the modern authentication horizon: Time-based One-Time Passwords (TOTP), SMS vulnerabilities, WebAuthn, and biometric passkeys.

Time ~10 min Win Implement TOTP two-factor verification and explain FIDO2/WebAuthn passkeys Mission link The cutting-edge standard for phishing-resistant web authentication

1The three authentication factors

Authentication factors are divided into three categories: Something you know (password, PIN), Something you have (authenticator app, hardware YubiKey), and Something you are (biometric fingerprint, Face ID).

Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) requires two distinct categories. Passwords alone are vulnerable to phishing and breaches; pairing a password with a Time-based One-Time Password (TOTP) or a cryptographic Passkey (WebAuthn) stops 99% of automated account takeovers.

Key idea MFA requires two different factor types; WebAuthn passkeys eliminate passwords entirely.

2How TOTP calculates 6-digit codes

TOTP (RFC 6238) hashes a shared secret with the current Unix timestamp rounded to 30 seconds.
