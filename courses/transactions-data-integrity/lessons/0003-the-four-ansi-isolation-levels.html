Lesson 03 · Isolation Levels & Anomalies

The four ANSI isolation levels

What happens when two people book the last airline seat at the exact same millisecond? Explore the four ANSI isolation levels and the concurrency anomalies they prevent.

Time ~10 min Win Select between Read Committed, Repeatable Read, and Serializable isolation levels Mission link Prevents race conditions and dirty reads in multi-user concurrent applications

1The spectrum of isolation

The 'I' in ACID stands for Isolation: ideally, every transaction should execute as if it were the only transaction in the universe (Serializable). But perfect isolation is computationally expensive and reduces throughput.

To balance performance with correctness, ANSI SQL defined four standard Isolation Levels: Read Uncommitted, Read Committed (industry default), Repeatable Read, and Serializable. Higher levels prevent more concurrency anomalies at the expense of concurrency.

Key idea Higher isolation levels prevent concurrency anomalies at the expense of throughput and locking.
