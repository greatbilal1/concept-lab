Lesson 04 · Event-Driven Architecture

Broker versus mediator event topologies

Should services coordinate themselves, or should a central conductor direct the workflow? Compare the Broker topology (choreography) with the Mediator topology (orchestration).

Time ~10 min Win Select between Broker and Mediator topologies based on workflow complexity and visibility Mission link Prevents loss of business workflow visibility in complex event-driven systems

1Choreography versus Orchestration

In Event-Driven Architecture, how do services coordinate multi-step workflows? There are two primary topologies: Broker (Choreography) and Mediator (Orchestration).

In the Broker Topology, there is no central boss: services act like dancers in a choreography. OrderService publishes an event; PaymentService reacts and publishes its own event; ShippingService reacts to that. It is highly decoupled. In the Mediator Topology, a central Workflow Orchestrator acts like an orchestra conductor, explicitly directing each step and handling failures.

Key idea Broker topology is decentralized choreography; Mediator topology is centralized orchestration.
