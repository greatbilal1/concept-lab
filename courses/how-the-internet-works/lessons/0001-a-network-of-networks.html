Lesson 01 · The Physical & Network Layer

A network of networks

The internet is not a single giant computer or unified cloud. It is a cooperative federation of independent networks agreeing to speak the same language.

Time ~10 min Win Explain how autonomous networks interconnect to form the global internet mesh Mission link Establishes the foundational decentralized architecture of the web

1The decentralized federation

The word internet is a contraction of inter-network. At its core, the internet is simply thousands of private, academic, commercial, and government networks connected together by agreed-upon communication standards.

There is no central master computer that controls traffic. If one transoceanic cable or major network node is severed, packets automatically route around the damage through other autonomous networks.

Key idea The internet is a decentralized mesh of autonomous networks sharing common communication protocols.

2The network tier hierarchy

Understand how home local networks connect through regional ISPs up to global Tier 1 backbones.
